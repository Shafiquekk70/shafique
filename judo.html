JUDO

THE GENTLE WAY

Judo is a modern Japanese martial art and Olympic sport created by Jigoro Kano in 1882. It emphasizes using an opponent's force against them, with techniques including throws, pins, joint locks, and chokes. The philosophy centers around maximum efficiency with minimum effort.

BASIC INFORMATION

Competition Format

One-on-one matches lasting 4 minutes (women) or 5 minutes (men). Can be won by ippon (full point), waza-ari (half point), or penalties.

Match Duration

Men: 5 minutes | Women: 4 minutes | Golden Score: Unlimited if tied | Children: 2-3 minutes

Main Philosophy

Seiryoku zen'yō (maximum efficiency) and jita kyōei (mutual welfare). Using opponent's strength against them.

Competition Area

Tatami mat: 14×14m (competition area) with 10×10m contest area. Safety area of 3m around edges.

SCORING SYSTEM

IPPON

Points: Full point

Result: Immediate win

Conditions: Perfect throw, 20-second pin, submission

Symbol: Red flag/scoreboard

WAZA-ARI

Points: Half point

Result: Two = Ippon

Conditions: Good throw, 15-19 second pin

Symbol: Yellow flag/scoreboard

YUKO

Points: Minor score

Result: Tiebreaker

Conditions: Fair throw, 10-14 second pin

Symbol: Blue flag/scoreboard

JUDO TECHNIQUES

Nage-waza (Throwing Techniques)

  • Te-waza: Hand techniques (seoi-nage, tai-otoshi)
  • Koshi-waza: Hip techniques (o-goshi, uki-goshi)
  • Ashi-waza: Foot/leg techniques (o-soto-gari, uchi-mata)
  • Ma-sutemi-waza: Sacrifice techniques (tomoe-nage)
  • Yoko-sutemi-waza: Side sacrifice techniques (yoko-guruma)

Katame-waza (Grappling Techniques)

  • Osae-komi-waza: Holding/pinning techniques
  • Shime-waza: Strangulation/choking techniques
  • Kansetsu-waza: Joint locking techniques (elbow only)
  • Transition: Combining throws with ground techniques

RULES & PENALTIES

Prohibited Actions

  • Attacking joints other than elbow
  • Striking, kicking, or punching
  • Wearing hard/metallic objects
  • Passivity (non-combativity)
  • False attacks (false entry)
  • Dangerous throws (kawazu-gake)

Penalties (Shido)

  • First Shido: Warning
  • Second Shido: Yuko for opponent
  • Third Shido: Waza-ari for opponent
  • Fourth Shido: Hansoku-make (disqualification)
  • Serious Offense: Direct Hansoku-make

FAMOUS JUDO TECHNIQUES

Uchi-mata

Type: Ashi-waza (leg technique)

Description: Inner thigh throw. One of the most popular competition throws.

Master: Yasuhiro Yamashita

O-soto-gari

Type: Ashi-waza (leg technique)

Description: Major outer reap. Fundamental throw taught to beginners.

Master: Kosei Inoue

Seoi-nage

Type: Te-waza (hand technique)

Description: Shoulder throw. Comes in morote (two-handed) and ippon (one-handed) variations.

Master: Tadahiro Nomura

Tomoe-nage

Type: Sutemi-waza (sacrifice technique)

Description: Circle throw. Sacrifice throw where judoka falls backward.

Master: Ryoko Tani

IMPORTANT FACTS

History & Origin

  • Founder: Jigoro Kano (1860-1938)
  • Founded: 1882 at Eisho-ji temple
  • First Name: Kano Jiu-Jitsu (later Kodokan Judo)
  • First Olympics: 1964 Tokyo (men), 1992 Barcelona (women)
  • IJF Founded: 1951 (International Judo Federation)

Major Tournaments

  • Olympic Games: Every 4 years
  • World Judo Championships: Annual (except Olympic years)
  • Grand Slam: Paris, Tokyo, Abu Dhabi, etc.
  • Grand Prix: Various locations worldwide
  • Continental Championships: Asia, Europe, Pan-Am, etc.

TEDDY RINER

The Greatest Heavyweight

Nationality: French

Born: April 7, 1989

Height: 2.04m (6'8")

Weight: 131kg (289 lbs)

Category: +100kg (Heavyweight)

Club: PSG Judo

Career Achievements

  • 10-time World Champion (2007, 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2017, 2022)
  • 2-time Olympic Champion (2012 London, 2016 Rio)
  • Olympic Bronze (2008 Beijing, 2020 Tokyo)
  • 5-time European Champion
  • 154-match unbeaten streak (2010-2020)
  • Youngest World Champion in heavyweight history (18 years, 2007)
  • Only judoka to win 10 world titles
  • Longest reign as world number 1

TADAHIRO NOMURA

The Olympic Legend

Nationality: Japanese

Born: December 10, 1974

Height: 1.60m (5'3")

Weight: 60kg (132 lbs)

Category: -60kg (Extra Lightweight)

Club: Tokai University

Career Achievements

  • Only judoka to win 3 consecutive Olympic gold medals
  • Olympic Champion: 1996 Atlanta, 2000 Sydney, 2004 Athens
  • 1999 Asian Champion
  • Signature technique: Ko-uchi-gari (minor inner reap)
  • Known for exceptional speed and tactical intelligence
  • Retired undefeated in Olympic competition
  • Part of famous judo family (uncle Toyokazu Nomura also Olympic champion)
  • Currently serves as coach and commentator
